Homecoming checking…

The weekend after discharge.

Dad came home from hospital with a letter that stops one medicine, changes another and adds a third. The kitchen still holds the old boxes. The GP is closed until Monday.

Photograph the letter, the boxes and the pill organiser. Homecoming shows exactly where the house disagrees with the hospital and turns each disagreement into a question for your pharmacist.

It never tells you what to take. Nothing is stored. Demo data is synthetic. Not medical advice.
